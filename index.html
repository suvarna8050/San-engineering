ABOUT SAN

Engineering built on decades of industrial experience.

Established in 1969 as a locomotive manufacturing company, SAN Engineering & Locomotive Co. Ltd. has evolved into a multi-product and multi-market engineering enterprise.

With manufacturing capabilities in Bengaluru and Mysuru, SAN Engineering develops and manufactures locomotives, power transmission systems, railway vehicles and specialised engineering solutions.

Its engineering capabilities combine design, manufacturing, testing and quality systems to support demanding railway and industrial applications.

SERVICES

Engineering support across the product lifecycle.

Application-oriented engineering support for selecting and sizing locomotives according to operating conditions, traction requirements, duty cycles and customer-specific applications.

Maintenance and technical support for railway and industrial equipment throughout its operating lifecycle, including replacement requirements, product assistance and practical service support for dependable operation.

Engineering support for vehicle upgrades, modifications and retrofit requirements, helping adapt existing railway equipment to changing operational needs while improving reliability, performance and service life.

Engineering support for gears, gear boxes and cardan shafts, with attention to transmission requirements, component integration and reliable mechanical power transfer across demanding railway and industrial applications.

Engineering and servicing support for railway vehicle structures, bogies and related assemblies, with focus on inspection, maintenance requirements, structural condition and dependable field operation under demanding railway service conditions.
